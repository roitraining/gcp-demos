# 3.2 · Volume and shape

*Part 3 · Consume — signals from histograms*

> [!NOTE]
> **Why you are here.** Latency tells you a turn is slow; volume tells you whether
> it is slow because it is doing more work. This page reads turns per minute and
> work per turn, then watches one prompt shape split the population in two.

The `multi-city` scenario names three cities in one prompt on a quarter of the
turns, so those turns make more model and tool calls than a plain London turn.
That difference should show up two ways: as a higher mean work per turn, and as a
second population in the `tool_calls` bucket distribution. All reads use PromQL
over `curl`, the path 3.1 settled on. Queries live in
[queries/volume.promql](../../queries/volume.promql).

**Step 1 — Drive the scenario.**

**Command:**

```bash
load/turns.sh multi-city 40
```

**Expected output:**

```console
NEEDS-RUN: load/turns.sh multi-city 40 — 40 lines, one turn in four naming three cities
```

**Step 2 — Read turns per minute and work per turn.** The `_count` of
`invoke_agent.duration` is the turn counter; `rate()` over it times 60 is turns
per minute. Divide the `sum` of `inference_calls` and `tool_calls` by their
`count` for the mean per turn.

**Command:**

```bash
export PROJECT=jwd-gcp-demos
export TOKEN=$(gcloud auth application-default print-access-token)
export QUERY='sum(rate({"gen_ai.invoke_agent.duration_count"}[5m])) * 60'
curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  --data-urlencode "query=$QUERY" \
  "https://monitoring.googleapis.com/v1/projects/$PROJECT/location/global/prometheus/api/v1/query"
```

**Expected output:**

```console
NEEDS-RUN: PromQL turns/min and inference_calls/turn, tool_calls/turn — multi-city run; mean above the baseline 2 and 1
```

**Step 3 — Read the two populations.** The mean hides the mix. Read the
`tool_calls_bucket` series by `le` for the distribution itself:

```
sum by (le) ({"gen_ai.invoke_agent.tool_calls_bucket"})
```

**Command:**

```bash
export QUERY='sum by (le) ({"gen_ai.invoke_agent.tool_calls_bucket"})'
curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  --data-urlencode "query=$QUERY" \
  "https://monitoring.googleapis.com/v1/projects/$PROJECT/location/global/prometheus/api/v1/query"
```

**Expected output:**

```console
NEEDS-RUN: PromQL tool_calls_bucket by le — a spike near le=1 (single-city) and a second near le=3 (three-city)
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **What it means.** The mean work per turn sits above the baseline of two
> inference calls and one tool call, but the bucket distribution says why: two
> populations, not one shifted average. The three-city turns carry the extra
> calls, and the latency and token queries from 3.1 and 3.4 follow the same
> split. Repeated work, not a slow dependency, is driving the cost here.

3.3 turns from how much work to how much of it failed.
